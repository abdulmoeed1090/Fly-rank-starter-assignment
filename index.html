Content Refresh Opportunity Scoring

FlyRank Machine Learning Internship Capstone Paper (ML-01 to ML-10)
Abstract: Digital publishers struggle to allocate limited editorial resources toward updating vast libraries of published content. This paper presents a machine learning case study addressing FlyRank's content opportunity scoring challenge: prioritizing web pages facing imminent search traffic decay. Using a 50,000-row working slice from the FlyRank warehouse (spanning 2025-01-27 to 2025-02-27), we evaluate a rule-based baseline against an advanced Tuned Multi-Lag Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDT) model. By incorporating backward-looking temporal velocity features—including 1-day/2-day impression deltas, position momentum, and 3-day rolling averages—the GBDT model significantly outperforms the baseline under a strict chronological 80/20 split and temporal leakage audit. The model increased ROC-AUC from 0.4622 to 0.6893, Average Precision from 0.2855 to 0.4756 (+66.5% relative uplift over the test base rate), and Precision@20 from 0.2500 to 0.3500. The resulting system is deployed as a decision-support prioritization queue accompanied by transparent reason codes and human editorial audit guardrails.

ML-01 - ML-03 1. Introduction & Case Study Framing

Modern Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and content engineering teams manage thousands of active URLs across multiple client domains. When search engine ranking algorithms update or user intent shifts, older pages naturally undergo traffic decay. However, manually auditing every page on a daily basis is cost-prohibitive. Content operations require an automated, data-driven mechanism to surface pages where editorial intervention will yield the highest return on investment.

This case study evaluates an ML-driven solution for the FlyRank Content Opportunity Scoring lane. Rather than attempting to reverse-engineer search engine ranking algorithms or automate content generation, our goal is strictly decision support: assigning each page an opportunity score that ranks decaying pages for human review.

Research Question: Which content pages should be prioritized for editorial review to mitigate future performance decline using observable search engine metrics?
  • Task Formulation: Opportunity Scoring & Prioritization Ranking. Rather than predicting an unweighted binary outcome, pages are assigned a continuous probability score to populate a high-density top-$K$ review queue.
  • Unit of Analysis: One row represents the daily observed search performance of one content page for one client (Grain: report_date + client_hash_id + content_hash_id).
  • Cost of Misclassification: False positives waste scarce editorial rewrite budget; false negatives allow decaying high-value pages to lose organic visibility unchecked.

ML-07 3. Baseline Action Score (Week-4 Reference)

To establish an honest evaluation threshold, a transparent rule-based baseline score was implemented using current Search Console metrics:

Baseline Score = 0.4 × gsc_impressions + 40 × (1 − CTR) + 0.2 × gsc_avg_position

Pages were mapped to baseline reason codes: LOW_CTR (CTR < 2% & impressions > 20), LOW_POSITION (avg position > 30), or MONITOR.

ML-08 4. Advanced Capstone Modeling (Multi-Lag GBDT)

Static snapshot features used in naive models fail to capture traffic momentum. We engineered backward-looking temporal velocity features derived strictly from historical dates:

  • impression_delta: 1-day impression change ($t - (t-1)$)
  • position_delta: 1-day search position movement ($t - (t-1)$)
  • impression_delta_2d: 2-day impression velocity ($t - (t-2)$)
  • impression_vs_3d_avg: Current impressions relative to 3-day backward rolling average

A HistGradientBoostingClassifier (scikit-learn regularized GBDT) was trained on 21,937 earlier observations and evaluated on 5,485 held-out chronological test rows.

ML-09 5. Validation & Research Claim Audit

A rigorous temporal audit was conducted to ensure no future lookahead or target contamination occurred.

Leakage Audit Verification

  • Excluded Target & Future Fields: future_date, future_impressions, future_decline (PASS)
  • Excluded Heuristic Outputs: baseline_score, reason_code, action (PASS)
  • Temporal Split Integrity: 80/20 date split ensures zero date overlap between training and testing sets (PASS).

ML-10 6. Content Action Playbook & Operational Rules

Model probability outputs map into human-trusted reason codes and review recommendations:

  • POS_SLIP: Position degraded by > 2.0 ranks. Action: Refresh Content & Depth.
  • IMP_DROP_VELOCITY: Impressions fell > 25% below 3-day average. Action: Audit Search Intent Shifts.
  • LOW_CTR_HIGH_IMP: Impressions ≥ 20 with CTR < 1%. Action: Review Title & Snippet Description.
  • DECAY_RISK: General multi-period momentum loss. Action: General Editorial Review.

Human Review Guardrails & No-Go Rules

  • Mandatory Human Audit: Editors must verify SERP layout changes (e.g., AI Overviews), technical indexing health, and recent refresh dates (< 30 days) prior to modifying content.
  • Strict No-Go Boundary: NEVER auto-rewrite live content using AI, auto-redirect URLs, or delete decaying pages based solely on model probabilities.

Monitoring & Retrain Triggers

Retraining is automatically triggered if Precision@20 drops below 0.30, target base rate drifts by > 10%, or routinely every 30 days.
