Content Refresh Opportunity Scoring

Track: Machine Learning & Analytics Internship Capstone (ML-01 to ML-10)

Executive Abstract: This project constructs an end-to-end Machine Learning pipeline to identify and prioritize web pages facing imminent traffic decline for editorial review. Using a 50,000-row working slice from the FlyRank warehouse (spanning 2025-01-27 to 2025-02-27), we formulated a decision-support scoring engine. Early heuristic rules (ML-07) established a baseline ranking. Advanced modeling (ML-08) introduced a Tuned Multi-Lag Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (GBDT) model utilizing backward-looking trend velocity metrics (1-day/2-day impression deltas, position velocity, and 3-day rolling averages). Under a strict time-aware split and temporal leakage audit (ML-09), the GBDT model significantly outperformed the baseline across all metrics—achieving an ROC-AUC of 0.6893 vs. 0.4622, Average Precision of 0.4756 vs. 0.2855, and Precision@20 of 0.3500 vs. 0.2500. Finally, an operational Action Playbook (ML-10) maps predictions into human-trusted reason codes (`POS_SLIP`, `IMP_DROP_VELOCITY`, `LOW_CTR_HIGH_IMP`) with strict monitoring retrain triggers.

ML-01 - ML-03 1. Problem Framing & Research Question

Content marketing teams face severe resource constraints and cannot manually review every published URL continuously. This project answers a core search intelligence problem:

Research Question: Which content pages should be prioritized for editorial review to mitigate future performance decline using observable search engine metrics?
  • Lane Selection: Refresh / Content Opportunity Scoring.
  • Task Type: Opportunity Scoring & Prioritization Ranking. Rather than predicting a binary yes/no output, pages are assigned a continuous priority score to order a top-$K$ review queue.
  • Unit of Analysis: One row represents the daily observed search performance of one content page for one client (Grain: report_date + client_hash_id + content_hash_id).
  • Cost of Wrong Recommendation: High false positives waste limited editorial rewrite resources; high false negatives cause decaying high-value pages to lose organic traffic unchecked.

ML-04 - ML-06 2. Data Contract & Signal Audit

Data was retrieved from the FlyRank warehouse (fact_content_daily_performance). Features are strictly derived from observed Search Console signals available prior to the prediction timestamp.

Signal Validation Audit (ML-06)

Empirical distribution checks confirmed that raw static search signals contain valid directional ranking information:

Signal Tested Hypothesis / Question Observed Finding Verdict
1. Search Volume (Impressions) Do higher-impression pages show greater click activity? High impression buckets (51+) average 0.619 clicks vs 0.022 for low buckets (1-5). CONFIRMED
2. Search Position Does search rank separate click performance? Ranks 1-3 average 0.497 clicks (0.0388 CTR) vs 0.0059 clicks for ranks 51+. CONFIRMED
3. Flag-Linked CTR vs Position Is CTR variable within identical position tiers? Within position tier 1-3, median CTR shows significant spread (1.04% median, 1.66% mean), confirming CTR anomalies identify sub-optimal snippets. CONFIRMED

ML-07 3. Baseline Action Score (Week-4 Reference)

To evaluate machine learning models honestly, a transparent, rule-based baseline was built using current Search Console signals:

Baseline Score = 0.4 × gsc_impressions + 40 × (1 − CTR) + 0.2 × gsc_avg_position

Pages were tagged with baseline reason codes: LOW_CTR (for CTR < 2% & impressions > 20), LOW_POSITION (avg position > 30), or MONITOR.

ML-08 4. Advanced Capstone Modeling (Multi-Lag GBDT)

Static snapshot features (used in basic Random Forests) miss short-term traffic velocity. We engineered backward-looking temporal lag features strictly derived from past dates:

  • impression_delta: Daily impression change ($t - (t-1)$)
  • position_delta: Search position movement ($t - (t-1)$)
  • impression_delta_2d: 2-day impression velocity ($t - (t-2)$)
  • impression_vs_3d_avg: Current impressions relative to 3-day backward rolling average

A HistGradientBoostingClassifier (LightGBM equivalent) was trained on 21,937 earlier rows and evaluated on 5,485 held-out chronological test rows.

ML-09 5. Validation & Leakage Audit

To ensure valid research claims, a strict feature leakage audit confirmed zero contamination from future dates or rule-generated outputs.

Leakage Audit Checklist

  • Excluded Future Fields: future_date, future_impressions, future_decline (PASS)
  • Excluded Downstream Outputs: baseline_score, reason_code, action (PASS)
  • Temporal Separation: Chronological 80/20 date split ensures no future dates exist in training sets (PASS).

ML-10 6. Content Action Playbook & Operational Rules

Model output probabilities map into transparent, human-trusted action categories:

  • POS_SLIP: Position degraded by > 2.0 ranks. Action: Refresh Content & Topical Coverage.
  • IMP_DROP_VELOCITY: Impressions fell > 25% below 3-day average. Action: Audit Search Intent & Competitor Shifts.
  • LOW_CTR_HIGH_IMP: Impressions ≥ 20 with CTR < 1%. Action: Review Title Tag & Snippet Description.
  • DECAY_RISK: General multi-period momentum loss. Action: Queue for Editorial Review.

Human Review & No-Go Boundaries

  • Human Verification Required: Editors must check SERP intent shifts, layout changes (e.g., AI Overviews), technical indexing errors, and recent refresh timestamps (< 30 days) before editing.
  • Strict No-Go List: NEVER auto-rewrite live content with AI, auto-redirect URLs, or delete decaying pages without manual editorial oversight.

Monitoring & Retrain Triggers

Retraining is triggered if Precision@20 drops below 0.30, target base rate drifts by > 10%, or automatically every 30 days.
